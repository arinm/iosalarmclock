Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Punctual (“the app”, “we”) is built privacy-first. We do not collect any personal data. This policy explains what that means.

Data we collect

None. Your alarms, labels, schedules, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device storage. We have no servers, no accounts, and no sign-in. Your data never leaves your device through us.

No tracking or analytics

Punctual contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not build profiles, and we do not sell or share any information — because we never receive any.

Device permissions

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): used solely to schedule and ring your alarms.
  • Notifications: used only to send local heads-up reminders before an alarm. These are generated on your device.
  • Calendar (optional): if you enable calendar auto-skip, the app reads your calendar’s all-day events on your device to decide which days to skip. This information is not stored by us and is never transmitted.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Punctual is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
